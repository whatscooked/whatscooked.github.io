Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What’s Cooked is a live food map. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Customers

  • Location: used on your device to show hotels near you. If you turn on food alerts, we store an approximate location so we can tell you when a favourite dish is cooked nearby.
  • Favourites: the dishes you heart, so we can send alerts.
  • Notification token: a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging, used only to send the alerts you asked for.
  • You can browse and search without an account.

Hotel owners

  • Hotel name, address, map location, phone/WhatsApp number, opening hours, photos and daily dishes. This is shown publicly on the map, because that is the purpose of the listing.
  • The owner’s phone number and login details, used to sign in. These are not shown publicly.
  • Basic counts of views and calls, used for the owner’s monthly report.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t process payments; you pay the hotel directly.

Services we use

Supabase (database and sign-in), Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications) and MapTiler (maps). They process data on our behalf to run the app.

Keeping and deleting data

Daily dish updates expire automatically at closing time. You can remove your favourites and alerts in the app at any time, or ask us to delete your account. See Delete account.
