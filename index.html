Questions

Is What’s Cooked free?

Yes. It’s free for customers, and hotels can list for free. We never take a commission on food.

Do I need to install an app?

Customers can use it straight from a link or QR code. Hotel owners use a simple Android app, which our team installs for them.

How do food alerts work?

Tap the heart on a dish such as biryani. When a hotel near you marks it as cooked today, you get a notification.

How does a hotel join?

Message us and a team member visits to set everything up in about ten minutes, including a QR stand for your counter.

Which cities?

We start in Lahore and add more cities as each area grows.
